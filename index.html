In Loving Memory of Ena

Words from her family

Ena (Risbey) Ardiel

Ena (Risbey) Ardiel was born August 6, 1932 in New Westminster, BC to ADA and Ken Risbey. She grew up on a strawberry farm in Mission, BC.

She married Doug Ardiel on August 26, 1950 and together made their home first in Bridge River, then Brackendale, then Fruitvale, BC. There Ena decided she was going to get a licence to drive a gravel truck, then became a paramedic and continued serving Ambulance when she moved to Sechelt, BC. Then when retired they wintered in Arizona for many years. They spent time in Mexico with Tim and Ruby where she sailed on a boat and swam with the dolphins! She enjoyed going to the pub for a pint or two with her granddaughter Monica. She taught her grandson Jace how to swim and many other special moments with her other five grandchildren. Then in her later years she lived in Clayton Heights care home.

Ena was baptized as a Jehovah Witness in __________ and enjoyed teaching about the hope she learned about in the Bible about the promise of God's Kingdom and the resurrection of loved ones back here on earth.

She was predeceased by her husband Douglas Ardiel, her brother John Risbey, her son Paul, and grandson Travis.

She is survived by her four children Tim (Ruby), Janeane (Alan), Caralee (Darcy). Grandchildren Monica, Jace, Afton, Nicolas, Breana, Ariel. Two great-grandchildren Xander and Zoey.

And many nieces and nephews who said she always smiled. So many memories of when we were all kids!

She was truly a nice person and those who knew her will miss her dearly.

Those we love don't go away — they walk beside us every day.

With love, always — Bubba's family
